About

I am a Ph.D. candidate studying Biomedical Engineering at the Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine. I work in the Pertea Lab in the Center for Computational Biology. Prior to pursuing my Ph.D., I studied at Carnegie Mellon University, graduating with a major in Electrical and Computer Engineering and a minor in Computational Biology. During the summer of 2022, I interned at Ocean Genomics, Inc., which is where I became interested in pursuing research in genomics and transcriptomics.

Currently, my work focuses on algorithm design and software development for processing and analyzing RNA-seq data. I am interested in transcriptome assembly and quantification at the transcript-level, which involves alternative splicing and isoform usage analysis. I am also interested in developing methods for new sequencing technologies, including long-read RNA-seq and single-cell RNA-seq.
